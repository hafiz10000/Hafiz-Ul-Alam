§ 07
B.Sc. Engineering Thesis2025

Optimization of Process Parameters on Nano-Powder Mixed EDM of Ti-6Al-4V Using RSM & Genetic Algorithm

Titanium alloy Ti-6Al-4V is prized in aerospace and biomedical implants but notoriously difficult to machine. For this thesis, we mixed Al₂O₃ nanoparticles into a sunflower-oil dielectric for Electric Discharge Machining against a copper electrode, running a 20-run face-centered Central Composite Design in Design Expert 13 to model how peak current, pulse timing, and nanoparticle concentration affect material removal rate, surface roughness, and tool wear — verified with SEM imaging at 500× and 1000× — then optimized the trade-offs with a genetic algorithm. The optimized settings improved material removal rate by 30%, cut tool wear by 25%, and improved surface finish by 20%.

With Larry Andrew Penheiro & Shoumik Deb Nath · Supervised by Prof. Dr. A.K.M. Nurul Amin
